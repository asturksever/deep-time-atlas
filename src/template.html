Built entirely on open data. Plate model: Merdith, A.S. et al. (2021), A continuous, kinematic full-plate motion model from 1 Ga to present, Earth-Science Reviews 214, 103477, with coastline and static-polygon shapes from the same model (CC BY 4.0, via the EarthByte group's GPlates Web Service repository). Rotations computed with pyGPlates and replayed in your browser as quaternions, interpolated between 5 Myr steps. Geologic time scale and period colours: International Commission on Stratigraphy, ICS chart 2023/09.

Note: the museum display uses palaeogeographic maps with reconstructed ancient shorelines and mountains. This version shows where today's continental pieces were; ancient sea level is not modelled, and longitudes before about 250 Ma are poorly constrained because palaeomagnetism records latitude only. Pacific absolute positions between 250 and 83 Ma are adjusted in the model.
